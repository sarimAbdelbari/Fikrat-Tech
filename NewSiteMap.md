### [COMPONENT: CaseStudyOverview]

**The Client**

Paradise Game DZ is one of Algeria's established gaming and mobile-services businesses. With over 50,000 followers on Facebook and an active, loyal customer base, they had clear market presence and consistent demand for their products.

**The Problem**

Despite strong demand, their fulfillment workflow was entirely manual. Customers browsed products on social media, then placed orders through live chat with staff. Payments were collected via CCP or Flexy and confirmed manually. Staff delivered products one by one through the same chat thread.

This worked — until it didn't. As the customer base grew, the manual process created delays, errors, and a customer experience that didn't match the professionalism of the brand.

**The Goal**

Build a complete digital commerce platform that:
1. Gives customers a professional storefront to browse and order from
2. Integrates Algerian payment methods without friction
3. Centralizes order management for the team
4. Keeps the chat-based relationship they'd built with their customers — but makes it organized and scalable
5. Gives management visibility into revenue, orders, and activity

---

### [COMPONENT: CaseStudySolution]

**What We Built**

**Bilingual Storefront (Arabic / French)**
A fully responsive storefront with RTL support for Arabic and LTR for French — both fully functional with a language toggle. Dark and light mode across the entire public site and admin panel.

**Product Catalog with Variation Trees**
Products organized into parent categories and subcategories. Each product supports a variation tree up to 3 levels deep — for example: game type → duration → tier. Every leaf-level variant has its own price, discount configuration, and stock behavior. Pricing displayed in DZD.

**Chat-Integrated Order System**
Customers browse, select product variations, see the final price, and submit an order. That order automatically opens or continues their support chat with staff. The entire purchase journey — browsing to fulfillment — lives inside one coherent flow.

**Real-Time Staff Chat System**
A fully integrated chat system for the operations team:
- One persistent chat thread per customer
- Real-time messaging (Supabase Broadcast)
- Full inbox with folder organization and message search
- Customer profile panel inside each conversation
- File attachments (images, video, audio, PDF via Cloudflare R2)
- Link previews in messages
- Quick reply templates for common responses
- Order completion and cancellation directly from chat

**Notification System**
- In-app notification bell for staff (new messages, new orders) and clients (unread chat updates)
- Web Push notifications for order status changes and new activity
- Permission controls in the navbar

**Admin Back Office**
- Product and category management (with images)
- Order management: list, create on behalf of clients, complete, cancel, delete
- Customer management with account status controls
- Revenue dashboard with charts: top products, user activity, time-based revenue analytics

**Role-Based Access Control**
Three roles: Client, Moderator, Admin. Moderators have per-module permissions (view, add, update, delete) across users, products, categories, orders, and chats. Admins manage full role and permission assignment.

**Authentication**
Email OTP signup with explicit Law 18-07 data consent. Email/password login. Google and Facebook OAuth. Password reset. Clients have a profile settings area. Staff are routed directly to the admin panel.

**Additional:**
- PWA manifest for mobile installation
- Full SEO: metadata, sitemap
- Optional Google Analytics integration
- Responsive mobile layout across all views
